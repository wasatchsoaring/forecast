Each day is ranked against the same site's HRRR history, within 3 weeks of the date. Tap a day for the hour by hour.

How it's rated. Thermal sites: an hour counts when the wind at launch height is inside the club's direction and speed limits, there's no storm or rain nearby, the thermals reach at least 1,000 ft over launch, and W* (average thermal strength) is at least 1 m/s. The day's score is the best hour's height over launch times thermal strength. Ridge sites (North Side, South Side, Burn Side, Poverty Ridge): an hour counts when the wind is in range, weighted by how close it is to the ideal speed.

Where the numbers come from. NOAA's HRRR model (3 km), read from the University of Utah HRRR-Zarr archive. Thermal top is the model's boundary layer top, capped at cumulus base. History runs from .

Known gaps. HRRR goes 48 hours out, so you see today and tomorrow. Wind limits for Oakley Ridge, Wire, Mahogany, Wales, Castleton Tower and Ken's Lake aren't set yet, so those use generic limits. A model, not a weather station: check live wind before you launch.
